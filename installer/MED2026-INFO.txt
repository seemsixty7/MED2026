MED2026 2026.0.1005a
====================

Release status: WORK IN PROGRESS (pre-release). Features and documentation are
still being reviewed. Test this build before rolling it out to production seats.

IMPORTANT - 3D models
---------------------
MED 3D models are approximations, not copies of manufacturer specifications;
users must verify all dimensions.

This applies to the conduit body / fitting solids made by MEDMAKE3D and to the
blocks in the MED 3D block library (MED3DLIB).

What this installer does
------------------------
- Installs MED2026 under the folder you choose (default C:\MED2026):
  Support, Data (catalog MED.db and seed data), Dwg (block library)
- Creates the MED2026 AutoCAD profile and desktop shortcut
- Optionally installs the Navisworks MEDProperties plugin
- Keeps existing MEDDataBaseSettings.dat, Project.dat and MED.registration.json

Restart AutoCAD after installing.
